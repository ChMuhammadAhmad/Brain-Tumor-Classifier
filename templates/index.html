Understanding the Four Classes

A brief overview of each tumor category the model can detect.

Glioma

A tumor arising from glial cells in the brain or spine. Gliomas are typically aggressive, malignant-like growths that require prompt specialist evaluation.

Meningioma

A usually benign, slow-growing tumor that forms in the meninges — the protective membranes surrounding the brain and spinal cord.

Pituitary Tumor

An abnormal growth in the pituitary gland at the base of the brain. Most are benign adenomas, but they can affect hormone balance and vision.

No Tumor

The scan shows no visible tumor mass. Healthy brain tissue with no abnormal growth detected by the model in this MRI image.
